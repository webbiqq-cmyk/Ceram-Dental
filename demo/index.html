This walks through what we sketched together: a public site where clinics start a case, an internal studio where your team takes it through production, and a portal where the dentist approves the mockup — all reading the same case record.

Services on day one

Protocol of acceptance

01

Website & case intake

Clinics browse services and open a new case through a guided form — service, shade, design spec, and the acceptance checklist your QC already requires.

02

Lab Studio (internal)

Your team's working view: every case as a card moving across Reception, QC, Design, CAD-CAM, Layering, and Photography — matching the board you drew.

03

Dentist portal

"My Cases" inside the website: live status, and the one moment that needs the doctor directly — approving or requesting changes to the mockup.

The case journey

Reception to ready-for-pickup

If the doctor requests a modification at the approval step, the case returns to Design — a note flags that additional charges apply from the second revision on, exactly as marked on the board.
About this walkthrough. It's a clickable concept, not the production build — enough to agree on scope before we write real code. The eight sample cases in Lab Studio and My Cases are illustrative; submit a new one from the website and watch it move through the pipeline live. A few items from the notes — the credits/billing model, and the access-control list under "Main Requirements" — still need a short follow-up before we spec them.
